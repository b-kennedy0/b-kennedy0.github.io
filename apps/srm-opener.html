Troubleshooting

> Only ONE tab opened / No tabs opened?

If you are unable to open multiple student records, please check your browser settings. Most modern browsers have a built-in popup blocker that may prevent multiple tabs from opening at once. You can usually find this setting in the browser's privacy or security settings.

> Email window did not open / BCC list is incomplete?

Check that a default email app is set up on your device. Email apps and browsers may limit the length of mailto links, so use smaller batches for long lists. Check the BCC recipients before sending.

Data Security

The student numbers and email addresses you paste into this app are NOT saved or collected by this page. They are processed in your browser only. Opening records sends the student numbers to UoC SRM, where you will need to sign in with your staff account. Selecting Email all passes the student email addresses to your email app in BCC. Only use student details you are authorised to access. If you'd like to verify this for yourself, click to view the source code.
